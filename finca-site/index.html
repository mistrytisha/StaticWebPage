The Place

Dining, grown outdoors.

Finca sits behind a brick archway in Sevasi, opening into a garden dining room strung with fairy lights and shaded by real trees. It's the kind of place that works equally well for a quiet coffee, a long dinner, or a table full of people celebrating something.

The menu leans comforting and a little global — wood-fired pizzas, sizzlers, fresh mocktails, and shakes worth saving room for — served at a pace that doesn't rush the evening.
